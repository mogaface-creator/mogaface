The Breakthrough

Why Generic Face Apps Fail in Clinical Medicine

Consumer AI face apps make people look like different humans. MogaFace is designed specifically for doctors and aesthetic clinics.

Dimension Generic Face Apps / Old Diffusion MogaFace Clinical Engine
Identity Retention ✗ Face-Swapping: Substitutes the patient with an unrecognizable generic model. ✓ 100% Identity Lock: Exact nose shape, eye shape, skull proportions, and brow arch preserved.
Melanin & Complexion ✗ Skin Bleaching: Western model biases automatically whiten tan/brown skin. ✓ Melanin Integrity: Zero bleaching. Natural Indian/tan undertones strictly preserved.
Hair & Scalp ✗ Hallucinated Wigs: Slaps pompadours or fake hair onto bald or shaved heads. ✓ Scalp Authenticity Lock: Bald stays bald. Natural curls preserved unless hair restoration is chosen.
Texture Realism ✗ Plastic Doll Blur: Over-smoothed, fake porcelain wax finish. ✓ DSLR Dermal Grain: Micro-pores, skin specular highlights, and natural texture intact.
Patient Conversion ✗ Novelty Toy: Leaves user confused with no medical next steps. ✓ Clinical Dossier: Generates a bespoke 2-page PDF report with WhatsApp booking.
End-to-End Pipeline

From Mobile Selfie to High-Converting Paid Patient

01

Client-Side Compression

Patient takes a photo on mobile. Our in-browser canvas automatically downsizes and compresses to <700KB, preventing upload timeouts and Vercel 413 payload errors.

02

GPT-4o Vision Scan

Multi-modal vision scans facial vectors, Golden Ratio symmetry, tear troughs, acne papules, forehead lines, and cervical angle in under 5 seconds.

03

Clinical Simulation

chatgpt-image-latest generates a high-resolution simulation with strict bone structure, hair, and melanin locks.

04

2-Page Luxury PDF Dossier

Vector-perfect PDF generated with pdf-lib containing the side-by-side comparison, clinical treatment pillars, and direct clinic WhatsApp consultation booking.
